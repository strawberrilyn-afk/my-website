My Hobbies and Interests ♡

My favorite hobbies include running, watching movies, and designing. I've had these hobbies since I was young, or was at least exposed to them, and I've loved seeing how my interests and perspectives on them have changed over time. Each hobby has become an important part of who I am!

A Few Things About My Hobbies

  • I started running in elementary school.

Running has taught me about dedication, teamwork, and pushing myself to keep improving.
